
Recent winners (e.g., Shree Anna/Millet marketplace) explicitly combined marketplace + traceability + price prediction + logistics, showing that a unified platform scores well when it solves multiple linked frictions + add procurement scheduling + status tracking on top of marketplace + price discovery

Core Features
Farmer registration: Mobile number, Aadhaar, bank account aur crop details ke saath registration.
Mandi map: Nearby mandis, distance, route, current price, queue, capacity aur open/closed status.
Price comparison: MSP, minimum/maximum/modal price, transport cost, commission aur farmer ka estimated net price.
Slot booking: Farmer mandi visit ka slot book karega aur SMS/IVR se token receive karega.
Live queue tracking: Waiting farmers, estimated waiting time, available capacity aur procurement status.
Digital lot record: Quantity, weighment, quality, moisture, grade, photographs aur rejection reason ka transparent record.
Online bidding: Verified traders, FPOs, processors aur government agencies transparent bidding kar sakenge.
Logistics support: Vehicle booking, shared transportation, cost comparison aur delivery tracking.
Warehouse option: Farmer nearby warehouse book karke produce store kar sakega aur better price par sell kar sakega.
Secure payment: Auction ke baad escrow-based payment, direct bank transfer aur payment-status tracking.
FPO support: Farmers apna produce aggregate karke bulk bidding, storage aur transport ka benefit le sakenge.
Credit facility: KCC, banks, NBFCs aur warehouse-receipt loans se intermediary par dependence kam hogi.
Complaint system: Weighment, quality rejection, payment delay, buyer delay ya excess commission ke against digital complaint.
Offline access: App ke saath SMS, IVR, missed call, CSC/FPO kiosks aur mandi help desks.
Government dashboard: Queue, procurement, storage, pending payments, complaints aur mandi performance ka live monitoring.
Demand forecasting: Farmers ko crop demand, expected price, arrival pressure aur oversupply risk ki information.
