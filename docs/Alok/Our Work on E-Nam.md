**Farmers side**—“Who will buy this quantity from me, at what price, and when?”

**Existing solution**—e-NAM already addresses part of this by connecting sellers with more markets and buyers and offering real-time price/arrival information and electronic bidding.

Real gap—**Is this actually my best deal after transport, handling, quality deductions and time?**

Government data shows substantial scale: **1,522 mandis and 1.79 crore farmers** as of 31 December 2025

Therefore your SIH pitch should never sound like:

> “Existing platforms don't connect farmers to buyers.”
> 

The jury can challenge that immediately.

Instead:

> **“Existing platforms have digitized market access, but our focus is the decision and execution layer that determines whether a direct transaction is actually profitable and feasible after aggregation, logistics, quality and settlement.”**
>
